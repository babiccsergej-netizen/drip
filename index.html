📖 READ BEFORE PLEDGING: ABOUT PROJECT DRIP

Our Core Mission: DRIP enables children (ages 4–16) to access sportswear regardless of financial barriers. We believe every kid deserves dignity, freedom of choice, and the confidence of feeling looking DRIP!

Why It Matters: The average wardrobe holds over 170 items, and nearly a third go unworn. Your unused sportswear sitting in your closet can give local kids their next game.

Where & How to Donate: Drop off your pre-loved, good quality sportswear (shirts, shorts, sneakers, club kits) at any local Jumbo store collection box or directly at your local sports club!
