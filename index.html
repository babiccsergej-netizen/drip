About Project DRIP

EVERY CHILD DESERVES TO LOOK DRIP

DRIP enables children (ages 4–16) to access high-quality sportswear regardless of financial barriers. Instead of traditional charity bins, we champion dignity, freedom of choice, and pride on the pitch.

1

Check Your Wardrobe

The average Dutch wardrobe holds 50+ unworn items. Look for clean, good-condition jerseys, shorts, tracksuits, or sports sneakers you no longer wear.

2

Find a Drop-Off Point

Hand in your pre-loved sportswear effortlessly at participating Jumbo supermarkets across Eindhoven or directly at your local sports club.

3

Pass It On

The DRIP Sports Van brings curated gear directly to youth neighborhoods so kids aged 4–16 can pick out their own kit with confidence.
